# Copywriting Frameworks for Ads

Read when writing new ads, designing A/B test variants, or reviewing creative quality.
One idea per ad — two messages = two ads.

## Voice of customer first

Before writing any ad, collect a minimum of 5 real customer quotes (reviews, sales-call
transcripts, support tickets, community posts). Extract: pain phrases (their words, not
yours), outcome phrases, specific numbers, emotional words. Use their phrasing in headlines —
it pre-validates the message.

## Six headline formulas (with funnel-stage fit)

| Formula | Pattern | Stage | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feeling | "X, not Y" | TOF | "Chi tiêu thông minh, không chi tiêu nhiều" |
| Conversation | Recreate a lived moment | TOF | "'Sếp ơi, trả lời khách đến 1h sáng vẫn không ra đơn...'" |
| Contrast | old way → new way | MOF | "Vẫn chạy quảng cáo kiểu 2023? Đổi sang cách 2026 này" |
| Shame | Status quo is outdated | MOF | "Ngừng đo lường bằng cảm tính" |
| Stat interrupt | One big number | any | "37% ngân sách quảng cáo đang đi vào giờ chết" |
| Pain + Outcome | < 10 words, problem → result | MOF-BOF | "Tắt giờ chết, giữ nguyên đơn" |

## Headline IS the creative (static ads)

- Text occupies 50-70% of the image area, solid background, legible at thumbnail size.
- Color mix of top performers: ~40% dark / ~35% bright / 15-20% light backgrounds.
- Numbers in headlines ≈ +20-30% CTR. Human faces ≈ +38% engagement. 4:5 aspect ratio.

## Hook rules (video)

- First 3 seconds decide everything: hook rate (3s views ÷ impressions) target 20-25%,
  below 15% = fix the hook before anything else.
- Captions always (~85% watch sound-off). 9:16 for Reels/Stories; < 30s for prospecting.

## Writing style guardrails (applies to ads, reports, replies)

- Banned filler: "unlock", "leverage", "seamless", "not just X but Y", rule-of-three padding.
- Lead with the answer. Numbers over adjectives. Short sentences.
- Match the audience's language — Vietnamese accounts: write Vietnamese ads, keep platform
  terms (campaign, frequency, ROAS) in English where practitioners use them.
